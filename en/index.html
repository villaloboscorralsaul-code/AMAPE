Have a special project?

Here are some of the problems we solve:

  • Line stoppages and downtime
  • Scrap and rework
  • Low productivity
  • Customer complaints and rejections
  • Line launches running late
  • Transfers that disrupt production
  • Product launches out of control
  • Inefficient layout and flow
  • Undocumented processes
  • Capacity that can't meet demand
  • No KPIs or follow-up
  • New engineers with no floor experience

04FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question? Message us.

Where do you work?

We are manufacturing advisors based in Ciudad Juárez, Chihuahua, and Guadalajara, Jalisco. We serve maquiladoras and plants across Mexico and the United States.

What kind of plants do you work with?

Any plant with production lines: maquiladoras and suppliers in the automotive, medical, electronics, aerospace and industrial sectors, of any size.

Can I hire a single service?

Yes. Hire one, combine several, or ask us for something tailored.

How do you quote?

First we get to know your plant and your challenge; then we propose scope, timeline and cost.

How do we get started?

Email us at alejandro@amape.mx and we'll set up a first no-obligation call.
